# Glossary

Domain terms and units used across the dashboard.

## Terms
- Barbotine: Ceramic slurry used in manufacturing.
- Silica (Silice): Percentage of silica content in the slurry.
- Covering Power (Pouvoir couvrant): Mass (g) vs time measurement from a plate dipping test.
- Viscosity (Viscosité): Flow resistance, in mPa·s.
- Density (Densité): Mass per unit volume, in g/cm³.
- pH: Acidity/alkalinity logarithmic scale (unitless).
- H₂O Flow (Débit H₂O): Water dosing flow, in L/h.
- Average: Arithmetic mean, shown as dashed red line in charts.
- Critical Zones: Highlighted ranges around compliance bounds.
